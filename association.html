Official Recommendation · 官方建議
🇹🇼
Bureau de Représentation de Taïwan à Aix-en-Provence
駐普羅旺斯臺北辦事處 · 120 Av. Napoléon Bonaparte, 13100 Aix-en-Provence
科技組 (Tech Division) & 經濟組 (Economic Division)
倘若貴團預計頻繁舉辦正式活動，建議您向法國政府申請成立 Association，以協會名義舉辦活動，未來在申請場地或與其他組織、協會、市府單位合作或受領補助時，比較不會有問題。
"If your group plans to frequently hold official events, we recommend applying to the French government to establish an Association. Holding activities under an association's name will make it easier to apply for venues, collaborate with other organizations, associations, city government units, and receive subsidies."
The Representative Office also noted that their Tech Division (科技組) and Economic Division (經濟組) are available to support Taiwan-related tech activities in France and to advise on establishing industry associations or chambers of commerce.
未來有需要辦事處或代表處協助推廣，歡迎再通知我們。在南法地區的活動也非常歡迎再來信通知本處。
Why an Association (Loi 1901)? · 為什麼要成立協會？
Legal Recognition · 法律認可
Formally recognized entity in France. Can sign contracts, open bank accounts, and act as a legal person independently.
Venue Access · 場地申請
Access to subsidized municipal spaces (salles municipales), university rooms, and institutional venues — unavailable to informal groups.
Grants & Subsidies · 補助申請
Eligible to apply for subventions from city, region, and national institutions. Opens doors to cultural and tech funding programs.
Institutional Partnerships · 機構合作
Official partner status with French Tech, MEDEF, universities, and — crucially — the Taiwan Representative Office in Aix-en-Provence.
Formal Membership · 正式成員制
Issue membership cards, collect membership fees, and give members voting rights — building long-term governance and accountability.
Media Credibility · 媒體公信力
Journalists, companies, and government bodies take associations seriously. Enables press releases, official statements, and sponsorship pitches.
